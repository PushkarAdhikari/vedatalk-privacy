Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how VedaTalk: AI Tutor ("VedaTalk", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our mobile application and related services.

Effective date: September 25, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use VedaTalk, we may collect the following categories of information:

Account information

  • Name, email address, profile information, and account identifiers when you create or sign in to an account.
  • Information supplied by a sign-in provider such as Google when you choose Google Sign-In.

Learning and conversation information

  • Text messages, prompts, responses, corrections, vocabulary, practice activity, and related learning history.
  • Voice or audio data when you use voice conversation or pronunciation features that require audio input.

Technical information

  • Device type, operating system, app version, language, approximate usage information, and diagnostic information necessary to operate and improve the app.
  • Authentication and security information required to keep accounts and sessions secure.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Create and manage your account and authenticate you.
  • Provide AI-powered English learning, conversation, correction, pronunciation, and tutoring features.
  • Save your learning progress and conversation history when those features are enabled.
  • Provide customer support and respond to requests.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, security incidents, and technical problems.
  • Measure and improve app reliability, features, and user experience.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. AI Services

VedaTalk uses third-party AI services to provide some tutoring and conversation features. Depending on the feature and production configuration, text and/or audio content may be processed by our AI service providers so that they can generate a response or analysis.

We do not use your conversations to sell personal information. We only send information necessary to provide the specific AI feature you request, subject to the terms and privacy practices of the relevant service provider.

4. Google Sign-In

If you choose Google Sign-In, Google may provide us with basic account information such as your name, email address, profile information, and a Google account identifier, subject to the permissions and settings applicable to your Google account.

We use this information to create or authenticate your VedaTalk account. We do not receive your Google password.

5. Data Storage and Service Providers

We may use third-party infrastructure providers for authentication, databases, hosting, analytics, AI processing, crash reporting, and other functions needed to operate VedaTalk. These providers may process information on our behalf and are expected to handle it according to their applicable policies and contractual requirements.

6. Data Retention

We retain account and learning information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide VedaTalk and maintain your account, unless a longer retention period is required by law. You may contact us to request deletion of your account and associated personal information.

Some information may remain temporarily in backups or logs after deletion before it is securely removed according to our retention procedures.

7. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your account and personal information.
  • Request restriction of or object to certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To make a request, contact us using the email address in the Contact section below. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

If you believe a child has provided personal information to us without appropriate authorization, contact us so we can review the situation and take appropriate action.
